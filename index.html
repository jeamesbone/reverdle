How to play

Normal Wordle hides the answer and shows you the colours. Reverdle does the opposite.

  1. You are given the answer word.
  2. You are given a picture: four rows of green / yellow / grey tiles.
  3. Find the guess word that would produce each row against that answer.

Type any guess and it lands on whichever row it paints — rows can be solved in any order. Wrong guesses show you the picture you actually made, which is your best clue and costs you nothing but time.

You are scored on the clock, which starts on your first keystroke and pauses when you leave the tab.

Every row has exactly one solution out of all 14,854 accepted words. No ambiguity, no luck.
